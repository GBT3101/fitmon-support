Effective November 2, 2026.

In short

  • Fitmon reads your activity from Health Connect (Android) or Apple Health (iPhone) to grow your creature. That data is used only on your device. It is never sent to the developer, to a server or to any third party, and it is never used for advertising.
  • The developer does not collect any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party SDKs other than the store's own billing library.
  • The app does not contact any server. The developer does not run a server for the app.
  • Fitmon never uses the camera, the microphone or your location.

Health and activity data

With your permission, Fitmon reads these records from Health Connect on Android (or the matching types from Apple Health on iPhone): steps, distance, floors climbed, exercise sessions, active calories burned and heart rate. Fitmon only reads; it never writes, edits or deletes your health records.

Fitmon uses these records for one purpose: to turn your real activity into in-game progress (experience, egg steps, credits, stat bonuses, quests and evolutions). To do that it keeps running totals in your save, such as steps and floors counted per day. It does not keep copies of individual records.

On Android, Fitmon can also read Health Connect in the background, so it can tell you with a local notification when your egg is ready to hatch or your Fitmon is ready to evolve. It can also count steps with your phone's step counter (activity recognition) while the app is open, or while the optional Walk Companion notification is on.

Your health data, and anything derived from it, is not shared with or sold to anyone, is not used for advertising or credit decisions, and is not transferred to anyone except as described under backups below. Fitmon's use of information received from Health Connect adheres to the Health Connect Permissions policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

You can withdraw access at any time in the Health Connect app (Android) or in Settings, Health, Data Access (iPhone). The app keeps working, but your activity will no longer count.

Your save

Your game save (your trainer name, the BOY or GIRL choice you made, your Fitmons, items, credits, quests and activity totals) and your settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device.

Android: if device backup is turned on in your Android settings, Android's backup service may copy your save (and nothing else from the app) to your own Google account, and restore it when you reinstall or move to a new phone. Android, not the app, makes that copy under Google's terms and privacy policy. The developer cannot see it.

iPhone and iPad: if you are signed in to iCloud, the app keeps a copy of your save in your own iCloud account using Apple's iCloud key-value storage. The developer has no access to it.

Bluetooth Link Battles

If you start a Link Battle and both players accept, the two phones exchange, directly over Bluetooth, what the battle needs: a random trainer id, your trainer name, the BOY or GIRL choice, and the battling Fitmon's species, level, stats and moves. Nothing else from your save is sent, and no health records are sent. Nothing goes through the internet. The other phone uses this only for that battle.

Purchases

Credit packs are bought through Google Play Billing or the Apple App Store. The store handles payment under its own terms and privacy policy. The app receives only whether the purchase succeeded; the developer never sees your payment details.

App stores

Google Play and the Apple App Store are provided by Google and Apple under their own terms and privacy policies. If you choose in your device settings to share diagnostics with app developers, the store may provide the developer with crash reports and usage statistics that it has made anonymous. The app itself does not include any analytics.

Your choices and deleting data

  • Fitmon works without a Google or iCloud backup; your save then stays on your device only.
  • Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on that device.
  • To remove the backup copy on Android, delete the app's backup in your Google account (Android settings, Google, Backup). On iPhone and iPad, manage iCloud storage in Settings, your name, iCloud.
  • Health records themselves stay in Health Connect or Apple Health, where you can view and delete them.

Children

Fitmon is not directed at children under 13. It does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.
