About the app

Fitmon is a pixel-art creature game that grows with your real activity. Your steps, stairs and workouts hatch eggs, level up and evolve your Fitmon, and you can battle a friend's Fitmon over Bluetooth. It runs on Android phones (Android 8.0 or later) and iPhone, with an Apple Watch companion.

Common questions

My activity is not counted.

Android: open the Health Connect app and check that Fitmon may read steps and the other activity types. iPhone: open Settings, Health, Data Access and Devices, Fitmon, and turn the categories on.

Where is my save?

On your device. On Android, device backup copies it to your Google account; on iPhone, it syncs through your own iCloud account.

Privacy

Your health data stays on your device. The app has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Read the full Privacy Policy.
